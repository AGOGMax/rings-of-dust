# Rings of Dust — audiobook cast sheet

For the ElevenLabs Audiobooks **Multi cast** → **Character Casting** step.
Detection is AI and misses minor speakers; check every name below is in the
detected list before generating. Dialogue counts are rough ("NAME said/asked").

Voice notes are from `TTS_Ready/LORE_BIBLE.txt`. Cole's callsign is
**Jackpot** — Heavy/Ricardo, Taylor, Federation people and engineers call him
that; Emily, Sharon, Gracie and Sam call him Cole.

## Narrator

One narrator for everything that isn't dialogue. Dry, unhurried, male or
neutral, no theatrical warmth. Prose is short declaratives — pick a voice that
lands a period without leaning on it.

## Rules

- **Two JACK-style traps here too:** iRIS and Gracie are the same architecture and must NOT share a voice. Sharon before and after the augmentation MUST share a voice.
- Cole is called "Jackpot" by some characters and "Cole" by others. That is correct. Do not "fix" it.
- No burn/attrition vocabulary is in the text; if a voice prompt or SFX label needs a word, use the book's.
