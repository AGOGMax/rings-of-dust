# RINGS OF DUST — Editorial Notes

Pacing and continuity issues flagged for revision. Chapter numbers reference the 29-chapter restructured version.

---

## Dev Edit Issues

1. **Emily disappears Ch7-Ch12** (six chapters after Sharon's death in Ch5) — needs at least one interstitial beat showing her grief and rage building before she resurfaces in Ch12 (Pier Nine).

2. **Cole's grief gets no oxygen** between Ch7 (Proximity and The Message -- learns Sharon is dead) and Ch18 (The Locked Bay) -- needs a beat between the wound and the reckoning.

3. **Conrad's graphite mark** (Ch2, Las Vegas) pays off 18 chapters later in Ch20 (The Secret Site) -- needs one callback before the payoff. Suggested: Emily notices it in the car, at the motel, or at the pier.

4. **The Rapture** (Saturn's closing window) is never established in any chapter -- needs to be seeded as a ticking clock before it becomes a plot constraint.

5. **Sam needs one POV moment** -- she is set up as the most perceptive person on the Golden Dawn but never gets a perspective window.

6. **DeMint reading a fabricated comms response** needs to land on the page before Ch11 (DeMint) -- even one Meridian interstitial showing a message that does not quite sound like Cole.

7. **Greenbaum's setup in Ch2** (elevator humiliation, Las Vegas) needs to be more visible so his return in Ch5 (Launch and The Ambush) does not read as a stranger with a gun.
