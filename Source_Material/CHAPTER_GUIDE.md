## CHAPTERS

### Ch01 — The Polaris
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch01_The_Polaris.txt`

Cole Nova steps alone into the Polaris's abandoned storage bay, moving through the dim hum of the engine room below to verify each of the 14 harvest containers. The ship feels vacated — most crew confined to quarters, medical teams on standby. It's the kind of silence that precedes something.

### Ch02 — Las Vegas
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch02_Las_Vegas.txt`

Dr. Stephen Conrad sits at the desk of room 1122 in Las Vegas, four hours before a conference he will not be the same person after. The glass of water untouched. The notepad unopened. On the desk: the Enterprise keychain — three inches of cast resin, his father's Sunday morning show, the object he's been carrying long enough to know it means something. He is going over the shape of what he's decided to do.

### Ch03 — A Thousand Thrills
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch03_A_Thousand_Thrills.txt`

Cole's origin story. The A-frame cabin in Sequoia — Ulysses Nova's hunting cabin, left to his favorite nephew, rarely visited. Cole checks the roof between missions, thinks about selling it, never does. What the cabin is and what Cole has been before anything started.

### Ch04 — The Colony
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch04_The_Colony.txt`

Jill picks up a transponder signal on a frequency not in any registry — not Federation band, not miners' auxiliary — three days out from Titan on a route she chose for speed. She follows it. She finds the colony. Most people find it the same way.

### Ch05 — The Faithful
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch05_The_Faithful.txt`

Day 5. Jill has her cells. She has not left. She tells herself it's the docking bay proximity, the food, the diagnostics she keeps opening on the Hound's Tooth without completing. All of these are partially true. The colony is in her now and she doesn't have the word for it yet.

### Ch06 — The Void
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch06_The_Void.txt`

Day 7. The ceremony is at 0300 — no announcement, no notification, nothing posted. Jill wakes at 0245 because something in the station's ambient sound has shifted. A settling. A stillness that isn't absence but attention. She goes. The Void-Walking ceremony enters her in the dark.

### Ch07 — The Crater
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch07_The_Crater.txt`

Day 18, eleven days after the ceremony. Jill is in the tracking room at 0347, an alarm she set and didn't tell anyone about. Tyler and Petra are already there. The crater is a focal point now — something the colony has been watching. Petra stands slightly apart from the data on the screen, the way she always does.

### Ch08 — The Pull

Day 23. A cargo contract notice has been sitting in Jill's queue for twelve days — Titan to Callisto, standard pay, pickup window closing in forty-eight hours. She's been reading it every morning and not answering it for twelve days. Now she has to choose.

It started as laziness. Ray converted a DarkX batch on a Tuesday — standard process, negative-pressure chamber, dense dark crystals. The containment window closed ten days ago and he hasn't submitted them. He's been looking at what he has. What he has shouldn't be possible.

## EPILOGUES

### Epilogue I — Dubai
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ep_I_Dubai.txt`

Marcus Verguld has been sitting at a corner table for ninety minutes — orange juice, four ice cubes, nearly perfect. He is reviewing the positioning errors. Taylor was one. The Panama files are noise in a structure he still controls. Then a second glass of orange juice with four ice cubes is placed quietly across from him. Not ordered. Already prepared. Whoever runs this establishment knew Klaus was coming long before Marcus did. Klaus: 71, unhurried. *"You built the cover. The structure continues."* A pause. *"The empire outlasted Wilhelm. It will need to outlast you."* Marcus has no heir. He has never produced a next layer of the structure. The ice in his glass has begun to shrink.

### Epilogue II — Nevada
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ep_II_Nevada.txt`

Taylor Glenn arrives in Ridgeline, Nevada — population four hundred and something, at the end of a two-lane road that gives out at the foot of some unnamed hills. He has been given the name Dale Custer. He holds the pocket watch through his jacket for most of the bus ride, the way you hold something when there is nothing else to hold. The hospital is a shell. Eleven people died when the supply chain collapsed. He knew the number — briefed on it as a logistics parameter, *acceptable attrition in the transition corridor*. He knows the phrase because it was precise and he had always respected precision. A man in a denim jacket: *"He wants it back."* Taylor hands over the watch. In the act of handing it over he understands the full shape of who sent him here and why. Not Marcus. Never Marcus. Marcus was the noise. The town closes in from the edges, the way towns do. *The machine disciplines its own errors.*

### Epilogue III — Sequoia
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ep_III_Sequoia.txt`

Late October. The first snow is on the high peaks. Rachel Vayes knocks once at Cole and Sharon's cabin. Cole opens the door before the second knock. She comes inside for thirty minutes — sits in the chair by the window, drinks coffee, thanks Cole for not dying when he was supposed to, thanks Sharon for coming back from something that should have been permanent. She asks about the arm. Sharon shows her the calibration. Rachel watches with the expression of someone who has looked at a great number of difficult things and learned not to look away. She stands to go. As she turns toward the door, her coat shifts — a half-turn, a breath of motion. The inside of the jacket is visible for just a moment. The patch is there: bronze star, olive leaf wreath. Both of them see it. Cole's eyes move to Sharon's. Sharon's move back. Nobody says anything. The door closes. Sharon turns from the window. *"It was always in the reaching."* She reaches.
