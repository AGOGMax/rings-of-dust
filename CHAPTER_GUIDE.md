# RINGS OF DUST — Chapter & Epilogue Guide

29 chapters + 3 epilogues. Four strands braided: Cole/crew, Emily, Ray, Jill/Colony.

---

## ACT 1 — Set the Stage (Ch 1–6)

### Ch01 — The Polaris
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch01_The_Polaris.txt`

Cole Nova steps alone into the Polaris's abandoned storage bay, moving through the dim hum of the engine room below to verify each of the 14 harvest containers. The ship feels vacated — most crew confined to quarters, medical teams on standby. It's the kind of silence that precedes something.

---

### Ch02 — Las Vegas
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch02_Las_Vegas.txt`

Dr. Stephen Conrad sits at the desk of room 1122 in Las Vegas, four hours before a conference he will not be the same person after. The glass of water untouched. The notepad unopened. On the desk: the Enterprise keychain — three inches of cast resin, his father's Sunday morning show, the object he's been carrying long enough to know it means something. He is going over the shape of what he's decided to do.

---

### Ch03 — A Thousand Thrills
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch03_A_Thousand_Thrills.txt`

Cole's origin story. The A-frame cabin in Sequoia — Ulysses Nova's hunting cabin, left to his favorite nephew, rarely visited. Cole checks the roof between missions, thinks about selling it, never does. What the cabin is and what Cole has been before anything started.

---

### Ch04 — The Cabin
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch04_The_Cabin.txt`

*Merged from: The Cabin + The Mountain*

The A-frame cabin in Sequoia, dawn to dusk. Cole splits the last log round in the crisp morning air, scanning the treeline out of old habit. By evening: Ricardo, Bitboy, golden light, bags loaded into the SUV. The end of the in-between before the world gets larger.

---

### Ch05 — Launch and The Ambush
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch05_Launch_And_The_Ambush.txt`

*Merged from: Arrival + The Drop*

Two operations the same week, cut back and forth. Cole's crew touches down — Bitboy jerks awake, doesn't recognize his surroundings. On a county farm road at 3:33 PM: Emily and Sharon in a parked Sprinter van, Sharon picking at a gas station sandwich, until a minivan pulls up and the Collective drop begins. Sharon is shot.

---

### Ch06 — Ray's Discovery
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch06_Rays_Discovery.txt`

*Merged from: The Discovery + Max at Alpha*

Ray's entire downfall in one chapter. It started as laziness — a DarkX batch converted on a Tuesday, containment window closed ten days ago, still not submitted. He's been looking at what he has. What he has shouldn't be possible. Three years at Alpha's materials research division working on something they called Dark Matter Stabilization and couldn't explain. The lazy thing through getting fired — one downfall, cleanly rendered.

## ACT 2 — Build (Ch 7–16)

### Ch07 — Proximity and The Message
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch07_Proximity_And_The_Message.txt`

*Merged from: Proximity + The Message*

Two Cole-strand chapters braided. Day 4 of the iRIS effect — no longer a voice, a presence that has learned the crew's architecture and calibrated to each person individually. Cole finds it faintly wrong in a way he can't name. Later, in the reactor bay at end of day cycle: Sam against the cold wall, Ricardo pacing, the ship's lowest hum underfoot. "You ever wonder what the actual shape of this is."

---

### Ch08 — Marcus
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch08_Marcus.txt`

Rachel walks Emily into the building without speaking. Lobby, elevator, three floors up, long dark hallway, two underlit Buddha statues, two men in dark suits who don't introduce themselves. Emily is brought in to meet Marcus Verguld.

---

### Ch09 — Ray and The Flywheel
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch09_Ray_And_The_Flywheel.txt`

*Merged from: Max and the Flywheel + Max in Panama*

Ray at his scarred oak table, the 180-day curve pulling him back — the flywheel problem that will become everything. Then movement: a flight Red arranged, three hours and forty minutes, a backpack that isn't in his name. Wakes to descent. He is going somewhere the map doesn't show.

---

### Ch10 — The Colony
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch10_The_Colony.txt`

Jill picks up a transponder signal on a frequency not in any registry — not Federation band, not miners' auxiliary — three days out from Titan on a route she chose for speed. She follows it. She finds the colony. Most people find it the same way.

---

### Ch11 — DeMint
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch11_DeMint.txt`

The containment building's subsonic hum resonates through Chancellor DeMint's bones. He sits in pale light, plucking at his beard, tethering himself to solid and controllable things. He knows more than he should and owes more than he can pay.

---

### Ch12 — Pier Nine
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch12_Pier_Nine.txt`

Emily checks the car the way Sharon taught her — perimeter walk, look underneath, check door handles. She does it methodically. The note is on the driver's seat: *Emily — Pier 9. — R.* She transfers her contacts to a new phone, wipes the old one, installs encryption, and follows the thread.

---

### Ch13 — The Faithful
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch13_The_Faithful.txt`

Day 5. Jill has her cells. She has not left. She tells herself it's the docking bay proximity, the food, the diagnostics she keeps opening on the Hound's Tooth without completing. All of these are partially true. The colony is in her now and she doesn't have the word for it yet.

---

### Ch14 — Saturn's Edge
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch14_Saturns_Edge.txt`

Cole sits in the smallest bunk on the Golden Dawn with the copper-wire ring in his open palm. The pebble still smooth. The wire gone dull green from years of handling. Saturn's rings outside — cold math, ice and particle distribution. He sits with the ring and the weight of what's coming.

---

### Ch15 — Surprise, Arizona
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch15_Surprise_Arizona.txt`

Emily wakes in a motel with carpet the color of a decade-old decision, not sure which way east is. She lies still and lets her body file its report. Two thousand miles of road behind her. The next phase begins here.

---

### Ch16 — Behind the Curtain
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch16_Behind_The_Curtain.txt`

Max examines the laminated badge: MIKOF FORSINKO, his face. Squirrel debriefs him — contractor database went live six hours ago, backstory clean, two Alpha-adjacent Sao Paulo firms both real and both made receptive. Max is going inside.

## ACT 3 — Peril (Ch 17–20)

### Ch17 — The Void and The Crater
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch17_The_Void_And_The_Crater.txt`

*Merged from: The Void + The Crater*

Jill's deep colony chapters in sequence. Day 7: the ceremony with no announcement, no notification — she wakes at 0245 because the ambient sound has shifted, and goes. The Void-Walking ceremony enters her in the dark. Day 18: the tracking room at 0347, an alarm she set alone. Tyler and Petra already there. The crater. Petra stands apart from the data the way she always does.

### Ch18 — The Locked Bay
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch18_The_Locked_Bay.txt`

Cole at the command console near Saturn's rings, no longer finding them beautiful — beauty requires distance. Sam at her station. Something in the locked bay that should not be there, and Cole's calm at the console that is not the same as being unafraid.

---

### Ch19 — Ray's Cargo
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch19_Rays_Cargo.txt`

Ray, six weeks into the Mikof Forsinko cover, walks his perimeter route to the park — learning the difference between how a man who belongs somewhere moves through a neighborhood and how a man who's watching it does. What he's carrying and where it's going.

---

### Ch20 — The Secret Site
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch20_The_Secret_Site.txt`

Emily on the dirt road at 6:22 AM, awake since four. The particular silence before something you've been moving toward for four hundred miles. The drive-in theater site — Vance's car sprays gravel. The vault is empty. DarkX stored deeper. The graphite mark from Conrad's hand glows. Gracie manifests holographically. Relay call to Cole at Saturn.

## ACT 4 — Crescendo (Ch 21–26)

### Ch21 — The Four Coles
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch21_The_Four_Coles.txt`

The cargo bay, quieter than space. Cole catalogs the quiets he has known — the cabin, the hospital corridor when his father died on the other side of a door. This is quieter than all of them. He is alone in it with himself, and there are more versions of himself here than he expected.

---

### Ch22 — The Boarding
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch22_The_Boarding.txt`

Taylor Glenn confined to quarters, but confinement on a ship this size is relative. He has his bunk, his console, a porthole that shows Saturn's rings when the ship's rotation brings them around. He does not apologize. He does not explain. Taylor Glenn does not do things like that.

---

### Ch23 — The Pull
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch23_The_Pull.txt`

Day 23. A cargo contract notice has been sitting in Jill's queue for twelve days — Titan to Callisto, standard pay, pickup window closing in forty-eight hours. She's been reading it every morning and not answering it. Petra sits with her and says the name: Von Grutzen. Jill hears it as her own. The notebooks are given. She leaves the colony with what she was always meant to carry.

---

### Ch24 — Vance at the Black Site
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch24_Vance_And_The_Black_Site.txt`

*Merged from: Vance at the Black Site + The Black Site*

Two confrontations with the machine back to back. Vance in the desert heat, steering with his knee, driving toward what he carries. The building is in Los Angeles — not offshore, not dramatic, a converted office building in the tech corridor west of downtown. Emily had expected something that matched the scale of two months' work. Marcus is gone. The door is open.

### Ch25 — Station Libre
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch25_Station_Libre.txt`

*Merged from: The Zero-G Flywheel + Max and the Station*

The zero condition confirmed at Station Libre. Ray arrives via a Collective transport that doesn't appear on any manifest — 2.5G burn, tunnel vision, arrival. Jill has upgraded the station. The full 180-day curve, in actual zero gravity. The flywheel is real.

### Ch26 — Rachel at the Station
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch26_Rachel_At_The_Station.txt`

The same 2.5G transfer burn Rachel took on the prep visit, and she braces for it the same way. She is back at the station for the broadcast — the thing that was always the endgame of what the Collective has been building toward.

## ACT 5 — Resolution (Ch 27–29)

### Ch27 — Grand Hall
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch27_Grand_Hall.txt`

The Federation Grand Hall seats 420. On the morning of the hearing, 412 seats are occupied and eight belong to the press pool, which has been told not to transmit until proceedings conclude. Five journalists complied. Three already transmitted. The hearing begins.

---

### Ch28 — Earth Return
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch28_Earth_Return.txt`

The Golden Dawn breaks atmosphere over the Pacific at 0617. Textbook entry — Gracie running the sequencing with six months of economy behind it, the hull speaking to the atmosphere in the language of resistance and negotiation. Cole has his hands on the controls. They are coming home.

---

### Ch29 — Cosmic Lanes
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ch29_Cosmic_Lanes.txt`

Cosmic Lanes — Sam's find, fog machines and black light, a carpet with constellations that don't match any actual sky. Six of them, seven counting Bitboy. Sharon is here. Cole sets the copper-wire ring on the table: "I don't think I need to replace it. I think this is the one." The ending that is also a beginning.

## EPILOGUES

### Epilogue I — Dubai
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ep_I_Dubai.txt`

Marcus Verguld has been sitting at a corner table for ninety minutes — orange juice, four ice cubes, nearly perfect. He is reviewing the positioning errors. Then a second glass is placed across from him, not ordered. Whoever runs this establishment knew Klaus was coming long before Marcus did. Klaus: 71, unhurried. "You built the cover. The structure continues." A pause. "The empire outlasted Wilhelm. It will need to outlast you." Marcus has no heir. The ice in his glass has begun to shrink.

---

### Epilogue II — Nevada
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ep_II_Nevada.txt`

Taylor Glenn arrives in Ridgeline, Nevada — population four hundred, at the end of a two-lane road. He holds the pocket watch through his jacket for most of the bus ride. The hospital is a shell. Eleven people died when the supply chain collapsed. A man in a denim jacket: "He wants it back." Taylor hands over the watch. In the act of handing it over he understands the full shape of who sent him here. Not Marcus. Never Marcus. Marcus was the noise. The machine disciplines its own errors.

---

### Epilogue III — Sequoia
**File:** `TTS_Ready/Ep_III_Sequoia.txt`

Late October. First snow on the high peaks. Rachel Vayes comes to Cole and Sharon's cabin. Sits in the chair by the window, drinks coffee, thanks Cole for not dying when he was supposed to, thanks Sharon for coming back from something that should have been permanent. As she turns to go, her coat shifts. The patch is visible for just a moment — bronze star, olive leaf wreath. Nobody says anything. The door closes. Sharon turns from the window. "It was always in the reaching." She reaches.
