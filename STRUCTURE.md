## STRUCTURAL NOTES

### Why 29 instead of 37
- Combined short chapters into one-two punches (Cabin + Ring, Launch + Ambush, Void + Crater, Pull + Open Condition, Vance + Black Site)
- Eliminated three-chapter single-strand blocks that killed pacing
- Colony chapters interleaved through Acts 2-4 instead of front-loaded

### Four-strand peak alignment (Ch 17–22)
- **Cole**: Locked Bay (18) → Four Coles (21) → Boarding (22)
- **Emily**: Secret Site (20) — DarkX, holographic Gracie
- **Ray**: Cargo (19) — proof goes public
- **Jill**: Void + Crater (17) — sacrifice, bloodline reveal, notebooks

### Key canon from this session
- **iRIS comms lockdown**: Taylor has iRIS fabricating responses to DeMint from launch day. DeMint talks to a fake Cole for weeks — his gut tells him the voice is wrong before the telemetry confirms it.
- **Taylor times Sharon reveal**: Sam gets the intel through the Collective back-channel (outside iRIS architecture). Taylor allows it to land at Saturn approach when Cole is maximally vulnerable. The grief is tactical.
- **Emily's DarkX encounter**: At the drive-in theater site, not Houston. Conrad's research already moved by Collective. DarkX stored deeper. Emily's nanoparticles from the Vegas figurine activate. Gracie manifests holographically — Cole = audio at Saturn, Emily = visual on Earth.
- **Disposal Protocol Eleven** (not Seven). "They named it Eleven. Which means there are at least ten others."
- **Dr. Hexo**: Bionic physician AND programmer. Built the hardware and wrote the software. Now rewriting both.
- **Vance survives**: Tactical vest, cracked ribs. Drives himself to a clinic. His record is read at Grand Hall.
